# Roadmap, PRD & Prototype (Module 4)

## Your strategic anchors
- **Persona (M2), who are you solving for?:** The Overwhelmed Viewer
- **Primary success metric (M3), your leading indicator:** Homepage-to-play conversion among Wanderers: the percentage of Wanderers who start content after visiting the homepage. A proposed target is a 10% relative improvement versus a control group. This directly signals that fewer users leave without finding something to watch
- **Moment of misery (M2), the specific friction blocking the goal:** User spends most time scrolling through titles and does not find anything that is appealing. Then user ends up closing the app without watching anything.
- **Guardrail metric (M3), what must not drop or break:** Month 1 subscriber retention for Casual Browsers and Power Users must not decline. Set a proposed tolerance of no more than a 2-percentage-point decrease versus control to protect the existing base
