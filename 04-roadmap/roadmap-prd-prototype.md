# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · StreamLine Spotlight

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** The Overwhelmed Viewer
- **Primary metric:** Homepage-to-play conversion among Wanderers: the percentage of Wanderers who start content after visiting the homepage. A proposed target is a 10% relative improvement versus a control group. This directly signals that fewer users leave without finding something to watch
- **Moment of misery:** User spends most time scrolling through titles and does not find anything that is appealing. Then user ends up closing the app without watching anything.
- **Guardrail:** Month 1 subscriber retention for Casual Browsers and Power Users must not decline. Set a proposed tolerance of no more than a 2-percentage-point decrease versus control to protect the existing base

## Roadmap
### NOW, 3-week sprint
- **A1 Spotlight Curated Rail**, A concise editorial shortlist directly reduces scrolling and gives Wanderers a clearer path from homepage to Play.
- **A5 Personalized Spotlight Queue**, A relevant shortlist directly addresses failed discovery, but recommendation quality and integration make it a major commitment for two engineers.

### NEXT, following 1-2 sprints
- **A2 'Why You'll Love This' Label**, Relevant explanations can reduce hesitation, but reliable AI personalization and hover alternatives exceed a simple label implementation.
- **A4 Mood-Based Entry Point**, Mood filtering can narrow choices, but requiring it at login adds friction and risks disrupting Casual Browsers and Power Users.

### LATER, backlog
- **A3 Hidden Gem Badge**, A quality cue may encourage unfamiliar-title selection, but does little to establish whether the title matches the viewer’s taste.

### ✂ Cut List
- **A6 Spotlight Digest Email**, Personalized email primarily drives return visits and adds delivery work without directly fixing homepage choice paralysis.
- **A7 Curator Profiles**, Discovering and following curators creates another selection task before the Overwhelmed Viewer can find something to watch.
- **A8 Watch Party (Spotlight)**, Group synchronization and chat require substantial work while leaving the core problem of choosing an appealing title unresolved.
- **A9 Advanced Filter Engine**, Detailed filters add decisions for overwhelmed Wanderers and better serve viewers who already know their selection criteria.
- **A10 Offline Download (Spotlight)**, Downloads address viewing access after selection, with substantial technical complexity and little direct effect on homepage abandonment.
