# Streamline: Future-State Journey for The Overwhelmed Viewer

*Four-stage future-state journey, built from the Product Health & Insights Summary and persona workaround analysis.*

## Context

**Persona:** The Overwhelmed Viewer. Goal: spend less time and get high-quality content that matches their interests in limited time, without spending more time searching than watching.

**Friction:** The user scrolls through titles, finds nothing appealing, and closes the app without watching anything.

**Strategy:** Reclaim operational simplicity by stripping away legacy noise and making the tool the fastest option for everyday use.

**Value proposition:** More user-specific content and suggestions, with lower-priced or free trials to win customers back. The risk is urgent because bad experiences and negative word of mouth can compound quickly.

## Timeline at a Glance

Welcome back → Decide fast → Watch freely → Stay engaged

## Future-State Journey

### 1. Stage Name: Welcome Back

- **User Action:** Accepts discounted trial, picks mood and favorites -> gets a personal shortlist instantly.
- **Internal State:** Wary and time-pressed -> relieved by a low-risk, low-effort start.
- **Pain Point Addressed:** 20-minute scroll of 15,000 titles -> replaced by a short tailored list (UXR-01).

### 2. Stage Name: Decide Fast

- **User Action:** Browses mood rows or describes taste -> finds a fit within minutes.
- **Internal State:** Confident and relaxed -> the choice feels easy, not risky.
- **Pain Point Addressed:** Exact-title search and franchise repeats -> mood browsing, descriptive search, varied picks (BUG-1080, BUG-1091).

### 3. Stage Name: Watch Freely

- **User Action:** Presses play on any device -> resumes exactly where they left off.
- **Internal State:** Immersed and trusting -> no reason to switch to YouTube or a DVD.
- **Pain Point Addressed:** Lost watchlist, restarts at 0:00, dropped playback -> synced list, saved position, stable playback (BUG-1058, BUG-1061, BUG-1042).

### 4. Stage Name: Stay Engaged

- **User Action:** Saves the next pick, receives weekly curated suggestions -> returns without searching.
- **Internal State:** Satisfied and valued -> sees the platform as worth keeping after the trial.
- **Pain Point Addressed:** Churn to hand-picked competitor emails -> weekly curation plus a trial-to-paid incentive (UXR-04).

## 3 Competitive Advantages Over the Manual Workaround

1. **Speed to first play:** One app, one shortlist -> a decision in minutes, not 20 minutes plus a detour.
2. **Personal without asking anyone:** Taste and mood drive picks -> no waiting on friends, book clubs, or emails.
3. **Low-risk, all-in-one access:** Trial pricing plus synced history -> no disc, second service, or lost progress.

## Caveat

"Minutes" and "short list" are design targets, not measured baselines. Before presenting them as commitments, validate with session analytics on time-to-first-play and scroll-and-abandon rate.
