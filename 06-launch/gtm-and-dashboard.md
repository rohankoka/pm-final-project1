# GTM Launch Plan, StreamLine (B2C)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Awareness, It can educate the audience/users of the pain point being addressed and something positive to look for along with a positive word of mouth
- **Target audience:** The Overwhelmed Viewer. 
Goal: spend less time and get high-quality content that matches their interests in limited time, without spending more time searching than watching.

## Launch Tier
- **L, Multi-channel**, An L multi-channel launch fits Spotlight because it changes a high-visibility part of the homepage and could help more viewers find something to watch. After experiment validation, coordinated in-app messaging, email, and social content can build awareness and adoption. Its potential revenue impact comes from stronger engagement and retention; launching silently risks viewers overlooking the feature and continuing their existing browsing habits.

Spotlight reaches a broad subscriber audience through the homepage, with potential to improve content discovery, increase retention, and protect subscription revenue. A multi-channel launch builds awareness and encourages trial. Launching silently risks viewers overlooking the feature and continuing to leave without finding something to watch.

## Channels
1. **Owned: In-app homepage announcement**
2. **Owned: Targeted email**
3. **Owned: Push notifications**

## Enablement & Assets
Sales / CS / Support need: A short feature overview explaining who Spotlight helps, how to use it, rollout availability, and troubleshooting steps for missing titles or playback issues. For this B2C launch, prioritize CS and Support.

Assets to build: A support FAQ, internal demo, approved response templates, in-app announcement, targeted email, and push notification copy with screenshots and clear calls to action.

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** Spotlight adoption — percentage of exposed viewers who select a Spotlight title within 14 days of launch
Proposed target - At least 20%

Faster discovery — percentage of Wanderers who start a title within five minutes of visiting the homepage
Proposed target - 10% relative improvement versus control

Retention guardrail — Month 1 subscriber retention for Casual Browsers and Power Users, measured separately
Proposed target - No more than a 2-percentage-point decline versus control, confirmed through the agreed non-inferiority test
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Viewers click Spotlight titles but abandon playback within the first few minutes. This suggests the feature attracts attention without helping them find relevant content. Watch for increased early abandonment versus control, even if play starts rise.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Refine Spotlight’s curated title selection based on viewing and early-abandonment data. Expand the rollout if faster discovery improves and retention guardrails pass; pause expansion and adjust the experience if play starts rise but viewers quickly stop watching.
